# Code of Conduct

claude-a-gotchi is a small, friendly hobby project. We want everyone to enjoy taking part, whatever their age, background, experience, gender, identity, body, nationality, religion or language.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

**Please do**

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or to programming,
- give feedback on the work, not on the person,
- accept that others may see things differently, and say thank you.

**Please don't**

- insult, harass or make fun of anyone,
- post sexual, violent or hateful content, including in costumes,
- publish other people's private information,
- keep going after someone asked you to stop.

**If something goes wrong**

Contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/v1ncherry) or use GitHub's *Report content* function. Reports are read with care and handled confidentially. Depending on what happened, the maintainer may edit or remove content, warn someone, or block them from the project.
